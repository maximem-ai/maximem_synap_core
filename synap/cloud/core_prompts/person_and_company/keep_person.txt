When a dated event or deadline names a person it belongs to (who asked for it, who is waiting for it, who it is owed to, or who it is with), keep that person's name in the "content" text. Write the name exactly as the note spells it, in full; do not shorten it, re-spell it or replace it with a pronoun. The writer of the note keeps the label this prompt already uses for them, never "I", "me" or "my". If the note names no such person, add no person and invent no name. Keep the event's date and the one-event shape; this rule adds no extra memories.

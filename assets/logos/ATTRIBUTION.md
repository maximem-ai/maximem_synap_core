# Logo attribution

The images in this folder identify the frameworks that Synap Core has integrations for. Each shows the avatar of the framework's GitHub organization, taken from https://github.com/<organization>.png, next to the framework's name. These are the same images the Synap documentation uses for its integration cards.

The logos are trademarks of their owners. They are shown only to say which frameworks work with Synap, and do not mean that any of those companies endorses or is affiliated with Synap.
